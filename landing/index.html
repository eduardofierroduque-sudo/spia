Frequently asked questions

What does SPIA do?

SPIA searches the internet for your exposed personal information — emails, usernames, phone numbers, full names — across social media, data brokers, breach databases, paste sites, and dark web sources. It gives you a privacy score and actionable recommendations.

Do I need to provide my own API keys?

For the Trial version, no — SPIA works out of the box using DuckDuckGo and public APIs. For Professional, you can optionally configure your own SerpAPI, HIBP, Dehashed, and Twitter keys for deeper results.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. SPIA is self-hosted — it runs entirely on your infrastructure. Your queries are never sent to our servers. Search results come directly from the configured public APIs to your instance.

How does licensing work?

You buy a license key once. That key activates SPIA on your self-hosted instance. Professional licenses include 1 year of updates. Enterprise licenses are lifetime.

Can I use SPIA for client work?

Yes. SPIA is ideal for security consultants, privacy auditors, OSINT researchers, and penetration testers who need to audit personal data exposure for their clients.

What's included in updates?

We continuously add new data sources, improve detection algorithms, and patch security issues. Professional gets 1 year, Enterprise gets lifetime updates.
